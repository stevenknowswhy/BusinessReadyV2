# Pill 4: Operations & Team

**Purpose:** Prove the business runs on systems, not on you remembering everything.
**Unlocks:** Sell path especially — buyers pay for businesses that survive the founder leaving.

## Have or Need?

- [ ] Business plan written (one page counts) — Evidence: the document
- [ ] Core processes documented — Evidence: SOPs / checklists
- [ ] Insurance in place (liability, etc.) — Evidence: policy declarations
- [ ] Team and roles defined (even if it's you + contractors) — Evidence: org chart / contractor agreements
- [ ] Payroll system (if you have employees) — Evidence: payroll records

## Done looks like

If you disappeared for a month, someone competent could keep the lights on from your documents alone.

## Make it fun

Framed as "clone yourself on paper." Each documented process is a piece of the business that no longer depends on your memory.
