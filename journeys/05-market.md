# Pill 5: Customers & Market

**Purpose:** Prove someone pays you and you know how to find more of them.
**Unlocks:** Investor and sell paths — traction is their love language.

## Have or Need?

- [ ] Paying customers / revenue history — Evidence: revenue records
- [ ] Ideal customer defined — Evidence: one-paragraph profile
- [ ] Marketing presence, one channel done well — Evidence: links / analytics
- [ ] Testimonials or reviews collected — Evidence: the quotes / review links
- [ ] Sales process documented — Evidence: the steps from lead to close

## Done looks like

You can show a buyer or investor real revenue, real customers saying real things, and a repeatable way to get the next one.

## Make it fun

This is the vanity pill — testimonials, reviews, revenue screenshots. The app lets you show off a little here. You've earned it.
