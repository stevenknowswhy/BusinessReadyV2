# Pill 2: Legal & Structure

**Purpose:** Exist as a legal entity in good standing — the thing funders actually give money to.
**Unlocks:** Loan, investor, and grant paths (all require a real entity).

## Have or Need?

- [ ] Entity formed (LLC, Corp, etc.) — Evidence: articles of organization / incorporation
- [ ] EIN from the IRS — Evidence: EIN confirmation letter
- [ ] Operating agreement / bylaws signed — Evidence: signed document
- [ ] Required licenses and permits — Evidence: license numbers / certificates
- [ ] Registered agent designated (if required) — Evidence: agent record
- [ ] Basic contract templates (client / vendor) — Evidence: template files

## Done looks like

You can show a lender a formation doc, an EIN letter, and a license — the three things every application asks for first.

## Make it fun

This is the "boring but powerful" pill. The app frames it as armor: every document here is something nobody can use against you later.
