# Pill 1: Business Basics

**Purpose:** Prove you exist. A stranger should be able to find you, contact you, and verify you're real.
**Unlocks:** Everything. No path proceeds without this pill.

## Have or Need?

- [ ] Business name decided and available — Evidence: name search / registration
- [ ] DBA filed (if operating under a different name) — Evidence: filed DBA form
- [ ] Business address established — Evidence: lease, utility bill, or registered address
- [ ] Dedicated business phone number — Evidence: phone bill / carrier record
- [ ] Professional email on your domain — Evidence: send yourself a test email
- [ ] Website live — Evidence: URL
- [ ] Listed on Google Business / relevant directories — Evidence: listing links

## Done looks like

Google your business. If a customer can find you, reach you, and believe you're legitimate — pill complete.

## Make it fun

First pill, first win. Completing this takes most owners under an hour and jumps the score fast — the app celebrates it accordingly.
