# Pill 3: Financials

**Purpose:** Speak the language of money. Every funder reads financials before they read anything else.
**Unlocks:** All four paths — this is the highest-weight pill in every readiness score.

## Have or Need?

- [ ] Business bank account, separate from personal — Evidence: bank statement (redacted is fine)
- [ ] Bookkeeping system in active use — Evidence: screenshot / login
- [ ] Profit & Loss statement, last 12 months — Evidence: the P&L
- [ ] Balance sheet — Evidence: the balance sheet
- [ ] Cash flow statement — Evidence: the statement
- [ ] Business tax filings current — Evidence: filed returns / confirmations
- [ ] Business credit profile started (DUNS / trade lines) — Evidence: DUNS number / credit report

## Done looks like

You can hand a lender twelve months of clean numbers without scrambling. That's the whole test.

## Make it fun

The app visualizes this one: a simple dashboard of your numbers filling in. Watching red turn green here moves the overall score more than any other pill.
