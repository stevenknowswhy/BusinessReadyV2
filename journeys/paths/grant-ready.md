# Path: Grant-Ready

**Destination:** You can apply to grants as they open — no scrambling, no missed deadlines.
**Prerequisites:** All five foundation pills, weighted heaviest on Legal and Operations.

## Path-specific pills

- [ ] Mission statement aligned to fundable outcomes — Evidence: the statement
- [ ] 501(c)(3) status or fiscal sponsor secured — Evidence: determination letter / sponsor agreement
- [ ] Program budgets built — Evidence: the budgets
- [ ] Outcomes and impact data collected — Evidence: impact reports / metrics
- [ ] Grant calendar maintained (deadlines tracked) — Evidence: the calendar
- [ ] Boilerplate narratives written (reuse across applications) — Evidence: the narrative library

## Done looks like

A grant opens with a two-week window and you're unbothered — the narratives exist, the budgets exist, the data exists. You just assemble and submit.
