# Path: Sell-Ready

**Destination:** A buyer can diligence you, value you, and close without surprises.
**Prerequisites:** All five foundation pills, weighted heaviest on Financials and Operations.

## Path-specific pills

- [ ] Business valuation completed — Evidence: valuation report
- [ ] Three years of clean books — Evidence: financials, year by year
- [ ] Customer and vendor contracts assignable to a buyer — Evidence: assignment clauses reviewed
- [ ] Key-man risk addressed (the business survives you leaving) — Evidence: succession / transition plan
- [ ] Growth story documented (where the next owner finds upside) — Evidence: growth memo
- [ ] Due diligence packet assembled — Evidence: the packet itself

## Done looks like

A buyer's accountant goes through your packet and finds nothing unexpected. That's a premium valuation.
