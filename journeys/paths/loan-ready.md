# Path: Loan-Ready

**Destination:** A bank or lender says yes — because your application answers every question before it's asked.
**Prerequisites:** All five foundation pills, weighted heaviest on Financials and Legal.

## Path-specific pills

- [ ] Personal and business credit scores known — Evidence: credit reports
- [ ] Collateral identified (if applying for secured lending) — Evidence: asset list
- [ ] Two to three years of financials prepared — Evidence: the statements
- [ ] Business plan with use of funds — Evidence: the plan ("here's exactly what the money does")
- [ ] Debt-service coverage demonstrated — Evidence: cash flow showing you can repay

## Done looks like

Your loan application is boring — in the best way. Every box checked, every document attached, no follow-up questions.
