# Niche: small retail businesses in California

BusinessReady niches down to small retail businesses in California — starting with San Francisco — rather than serving all small businesses generically. We chose this because retail has roughly double the compliance surface of a SaaS business (seller's permits, Planning/zoning, formula retail review, signage, health permits, SF labor ordinances, property statements), most of it city-specific and most of it blocking: you cannot open the doors without it. Compliance density is the wedge — it's where a guided, proactive app earns its keep and where generic go-to-market apps are too shallow to help. The niche also sharpens every pill: each requirement maps to a real agency, a real form, and a real penalty for missing it.
