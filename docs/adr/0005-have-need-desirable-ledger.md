# The ledger is HAVE / NEED / DESIRABLE

Every readiness item in the app is HAVE, NEED, or DESIRABLE. This supersedes ADR-0002's "no third state" — the two-state ledger forced aspirational items into NEED, where they dragged the score down for things the business was never required to have.

- **HAVE**: the business satisfies the item, with evidence attached. Claims without evidence don't count — HAVE unlocks only with an uploaded document or photo, a valid URL, or a permit/filing identifier (or another inspectable record). This is enforced, not honor-system.
- **NEED**: the item is required for readiness on the chosen path and still missing. Always paired with a concrete next action.
- **DESIRABLE**: the item would strengthen the business but isn't required for readiness on the chosen path. Tracked, no next action required, no evidence required — aspirational by definition.

The readiness score is HAVE ÷ (HAVE + NEED). DESIRABLE is excluded from the score entirely, so the number stays a measure of what's actually required. The score moves only when reality moves.

Decided by Stefano, 2026-09-29. Spelling canon: DESIRABLE.
