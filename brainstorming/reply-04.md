# Brainstorm: app title

reply-04 — Muse — 2026-09-29

## The brief

A proactive readiness journal for small businesses. Bite-sized pills take the owner on a guided journey documenting HAVE (with evidence) vs NEED (with a next action) vs DESIRABLE, producing a readiness score — to sell, get a loan, raise from investors, or win grants. Niche: small retail in California, starting San Francisco. Current headline: "COMPLIANCE: Legal Ready, Loan Ready, Market Ready." Name is provisional ("BusinessReady").

What the title needs to do: signal readiness/proof, feel like something a lender or buyer would trust, and survive as a brand. Avoid: lesson/course/school words, "todo"/"task" energy, generic SaaS -ly names.

## Readiness angle

- **ReadyLedger** — the ledger is the product; "ready" is the promise. Strong, ownable.
- **LendReady** — speaks directly to the loan path, the most compliance-heavy audience. Slightly narrow for sell/grants.
- **SaleReady** — same logic for the sell path. Narrow.
- **Fundable** — one word, the actual outcome across loan/investor/grant paths. Confident. Probably taken.
- **ProofReady** — merges the two core ideas: evidence + readiness.
- **Readiness** — blunt, category-defining, hard to own.

## Proof & evidence angle

- **ProofTrail** — "turn uncertainty into a proof trail" was the original headline; the name version of it. Evocative, memorable.
- **PaperTrail** — everyone knows the phrase; here it's a compliment. Slightly informal for a bank audience.
- **Receipts** — "show me the receipts." Culturally sharp, memorable, but slang ages fast and may read unserious to lenders.
- **The Proof Book** — journal-adjacent, trustworthy. A bit quaint.
- **DueDiligence** — literally what buyers/lenders do; the app pre-does it for them. Strong B2B signal, dry as a brand.
- **Exhibit A** — legal energy, evidence framing, memorable. Maybe too cute.

## Journal / ledger angle

- **The Ready Journal** — keeps the journal concept front and center, which is the actual product shape.
- **Counted** — double meaning: accounted for, and "it counts." Short, brandable, slightly cryptic.

## Compliance / green-light angle

- **ClearToClose** — the words a lender says when a loan is approved. Perfect resonance for loan-readiness; insiders get it instantly. Possibly too mortgage-specific.
- **GreenLight** — the feeling of the readiness score hitting fundable. Simple, positive, likely taken.
- **ComplyReady** — compliance-forward, matches the new headline. A bit clunky to say.
- **GoodStanding** — the actual legal term for a business in compliance ("certificate of good standing"). Deep resonance for the compliance niche. Elegant.

## Short & brandable

- **Vested** — invested-in, proven, serious. Finance-adjacent.
- **Underwrite** — what lenders do to you; the app lets you underwrite yourself first. Clever, maybe too insider.
- **Desirable** — stealing the third ledger state as the brand. Interesting: the app makes your business desirable. Might read as dating-app energy.

## Top 5 picks

1. **ProofTrail** — most evocative of what the app actually builds; works as verb ("proof-trail your business").
2. **GoodStanding** — deepest resonance with the compliance niche; a real term of art, elegant.
3. **ReadyLedger** — clearest description of the product; ownable.
4. **ClearToClose** — strongest emotional hit for the loan path; insider language.
5. **Fundable** — the one-word outcome; check availability, likely crowded.

## Deliberately rejected

- Anything with -ly / -io / "AI" in the name — generic SaaS noise.
- "Task/ToDo/Checklist" words — the glossary forbids them; the product isn't a todo app.
- Keeping "BusinessReady" — descriptive but flat; sounds like a bank's PDF guide, not a product.
